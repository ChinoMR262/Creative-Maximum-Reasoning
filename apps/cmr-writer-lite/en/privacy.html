Who is behind the app

Jonathan Gabriel Nieto · CMR — Creative Maximum Reasoning

This page explains plainly what information CMR Writer Lite handles and what happens to your writing.

The app is designed to support your writing and organize your ideas locally.

1. What is stored

  • Projects, chapters, characters, places, objects, timelines, and text formatting created in the app.
  • Images and text added to covers, the end of a work, or chapters.
  • Local preferences, including your language and the narrative colors you choose for characters, places, and objects.
  • Notes, drafts, and the date of any reminder you create.
  • The name, photo, and frame you choose for your local writer profile.
  • Temporary character-profile drafts while you complete them. They stay in private storage so they can be recovered if Android closes the screen and are deleted when you save the profile.
  • .cmr backups when you choose to create, import, save, or share them.
  • TXT or ZIP files when you choose to export a chapter or work.
  • PDFs you choose to import as a manuscript. The app uses a private temporary copy to read selectable text and, if you accept it, local OCR for scanned pages. The copy is deleted when the import attempt ends.

In the current version, this information stays on your device or wherever you choose to save it. Your writing is not sent to CMR by default. Images are selected through the system picker and the app keeps available resolution up to 4K. Only source files larger than 120 MB are rejected to protect phone memory.

A temporary character draft is not sent online or included in a backup until you save the profile.

Your profile photo is copied to the app's private storage and resized to a reasonable size to avoid unnecessary memory use. It is not uploaded to CMR, used for advertising, or included in your projects' .cmr backups. You can remove it from the profile; it is also deleted when you delete the profile or uninstall the app.

PDF reading happens on the device. CMR does not remove passwords, encryption, DRM, or other file protections, and it does not send your manuscript to a server to import it.

A TXT or ZIP export contains only the ordered manuscript. It does not include a cover, back cover, images, records, timeline, or a backup. Use PDF or a .cmr copy to preserve the work with its images, depending on what you need.

When saving a backup, CMR first creates a copy in the local Mis Proyectos folder inside the app's private storage. You may then save another copy wherever you choose. That folder is not shared or uploaded to servers and can be removed with local data when the app is uninstalled.

If an import will replace existing data, CMR first creates a temporary copy in its private storage. It is deleted after a successful import. If the process stops early, it may remain as a technical recovery copy inside the app; it is not shared or published.

When a backup belongs to a compatible earlier version, the adaptation happens locally on an in-memory copy. CMR does not modify the file you chose and shows a report of what was recovered and what was unavailable until you close it yourself.

If you enable a reminder, Android schedules a local notification. CMR explains why before asking permission to show notifications. If you decline, the note is still saved. The note and date are not sent to a server, and Android may delay the notification by a few minutes to protect battery life. If you import the backup on another phone, the date is recovered but the alert must be enabled there again.

2. Official distribution audience

The official Google Play distribution of CMR Writer Lite is configured for people aged 18 and over. The app does not ask for or verify the age of people who use it and is not designed as a children's experience. If the target audience, ads, purchases, or data handling changes, this policy and the Google Play declaration will be updated before that version is distributed.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

The app does not require an account or cloud synchronization. The free version may display a discreet Google Mobile Ads banner; CMR Writer Pro removes it. That SDK may process device identifiers, technical information, IP address, and signals needed to serve, limit, or measure ads, according to its configuration, applicable consent, and Google policies.

Closed-test builds use Google's test ad identifiers. The commercial ad unit is reserved for a public monetized release and is not published on this site, in backups, or in open source code. CMR does not use the content of your projects, chapters, images, or notes to create its own advertising.

Where applicable, Google's privacy message lets people consent, decline, or manage advertising choices. The app requests consent information at launch and shows Ad preferences in Settings only when Google says that entry point must be available. CMR does not keep its own copy of that decision.

CMR does not create its own profiles or use its own analytics. It also does not request location, contacts, SMS, calls, camera, or microphone access.

During a closed test, CMR may invite you outside the app to complete a voluntary feedback form. That form does not create an account in CMR or receive manuscripts, backups, or files. If you agree to a specific follow-up question and leave an email address, it is used only to understand your comment; it is not used for advertising or added to the app's local data.

When CMR Writer Pro is available, its one-time purchase is processed through Google Play. CMR does not receive your card details or manage billing. The app stores Pro access locally so the purchased feature can keep working offline.

CMR may ask Google Play whether a newer version is available. For that check, Google Play Core may process technical device information, the installed app version, and app modules. CMR does not send projects, chapters, images, notes, or backups during this check. If there is no connection or Google Play does not respond, the local app keeps working.

4. When you share a file

If you share a backup, TXT, or ZIP, Android delivers it to the app, service, or person you select. From then on, that third party may handle the file under its own rules. CMR cannot control or delete copies that leave your device.

5. Why we use information

Information is used so you can write, organize projects, preserve preferences, create backups, import files, export text, and recover work locally. We do not sell or rent your content.

6. How to delete it

You may delete projects in the app and remove backups, TXT files, or ZIPs from the location where you saved them. When you uninstall the app, Android removes local data under its own rules; this does not delete files you exported or shared outside private storage.

7. Security

Local content depends on the protection of your device and operating system. .cmr backups are not an encrypted vault: keep them carefully and avoid sharing them with people or services you do not know.

8. Changes and questions

If a future version changes how information is handled, this policy and its date will be updated. Purchases will follow Google Play's terms and data handling. If you have a question about privacy or your local data, contact cmr.apps.oficial@gmail.com.
